Our
Story.

Celebrating the sonic heritage of iconic audio players — keeping the warmth alive in a world of cold digital playback.

Byron Statics believes in enjoying unique and retro audio experiences through the archetypal players of the years.

In a world saturated with cold, sterile digital playback, we celebrate the distinctive sounds and undeniable style of iconic audio formats. From the warm crackle of a cassette tape to the unmistakable mechanical precision of a top-loading CD player, our collection brings back the rich character that defined generations of music listening.

Each product in our lineup — from our portable boomboxes to our vintage-style radios — is designed with both authentic retro aesthetics and modern versatility. We believe that high-quality audio and timeless design are not mutually exclusive; they enhance each other.

Whether you're studying, hosting a family gathering, enjoying personal leisure time, or taking music outdoors, our players are built to be your companion. Portable, dependable, and unmistakably retro.
