# Guest Post Outreach for ByronStatics

**Why guest posts:** A single guest post on a DR 50+ site is worth more than 100 social media shares. These are real, durable, hard-to-replicate backlinks that move the SEO needle.

**Reality check:** Guest post outreach is a numbers game. Expect a 5-15% reply rate and a 1-5% placement rate. Plan on 50-100 pitches to land 5-10 placements.

## Email finding methodology

### Free methods
- **Hunter.io** (free tier) — find emails by domain
- **Voila Norbert** (free credits) — find anyone's email
- **Manual search:** "author name" + "email" or "contact"
- **Check the site's "About" / "Contact" / "Write for us" page**
- **Twitter DMs** (sometimes faster than email)

### Paid (if budget allows)
- **Ahrefs Content Explorer** — find sites that wrote about similar topics
- **BuzzStream** — outreach management
- **Pitchbox** — agency-grade outreach

### Email format guesses (50% hit rate)
For most sites, try:
- firstname@domain.com
- firstname.lastname@domain.com
- tips@domain.com
- editorial@domain.com
- hello@domain.com

- "Story idea: Why CD boombox sales just hit +47% YoY"
- "Pitch: The 2026 retro audio boom, in numbers"

Brian
ByronStatics
info@thelivingenrichment.com

## Pitch subject line A/B test ideas

Test these 5 subject lines and track open rates:
1. "Story idea: [specific data point]"
2. "Quick question about [site name]'s coverage of [topic]"
3. "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"
4. "Free [product] for review"
5. "Following up on your piece about [article they wrote]"

Subject line best practices:
- 6-10 words
- Specific (mention their site by name)
- Personal (not generic "Hello Webmaster")
- Curiosity-driven (not clickbait)
- ALL LOWERCASE for personal feel (controversial — test it)

## What to offer in exchange

For most sites, the value exchange is:
- **Free, original content** (1,000-1,500 words, well-researched)
- **Original photography** (high-res product shots)
- **First-party data** (rare and valuable)
- **Founder/expert interview availability**

For very high-DR sites (Wired, Verge, Fast Company), you may need to:
- Pitch the story idea only (they assign their own writers)
- Offer exclusive access to a product
- Offer co-branded content (their name, your data)

For niche audio sites:
- Free product is the most effective
- A 5-minute founder interview
- Affiliate revenue share (some sites have affiliate programs)

## Realistic expectations

### 50 pitches sent
- 30-40 opened (~60-80% open rate with good subject line)
- 5-10 replies (~10-20% reply rate)
- 2-5 placements (~4-10% placement rate)
- 1-2 high-quality placements (DR 60+, real audience)

### Quality > quantity
A single link from Wired (DR 93) is worth more than 50 links from DR 20 blogs. Prioritize:

1. **Domain authority** (DR 50+ minimum)
2. **Relevant audience** (audio, design, retro, lifestyle)
3. **Real readership** (check if they have comments, social shares)
4. **Editorial standards** (do they publish thoughtful content? or just listicles?)

### Red flags (skip these)
- Site accepts any content for $50 (link farm)
- Site has 100% outbound link ratio (clearly selling links)
- No actual readership (no comments, no social shares)
- Unrelated niche (will look spammy to Google)
- Penalized site (check with Ahrefs/Semrush)
