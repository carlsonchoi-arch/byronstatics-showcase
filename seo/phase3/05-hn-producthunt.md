# Hacker News + Product Hunt Launch for ByronStatics

**Why these platforms:** One viral launch on either platform can drive 50K-200K visitors in a single day. They have highly technical, design-aware audiences that match ByronStatics' positioning perfectly.

**Realistic expectations:**
- Hacker News: 5-15% chance of front page, 80% chance of buried post
- Product Hunt: 70% chance of making Top 10 in your category, 30% chance of Top 5 overall
- Worst case: 200-500 visitors + 1-2 backlinks
- Best case: 50K+ visitors, 20+ backlinks, 1-2 podcast/press interview requests

---

## Hacker News launch

### When to post
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 8:00-9:00 AM Pacific Time (peak US East Coast morning)
- **Avoid:** Mondays, Fridays, weekends, US holidays
- **Best time of year:** avoid December (low traffic), avoid big tech news days

### Account prep
- HN account needs 6+ months of history to avoid "new account" flag
- Build karma first by commenting on other posts
- If you don't have an HN account with karma, use a personal account with a brief bio
- DO NOT use a brand-named account

### The "Show HN" format

HN has a specific format: title starts with "Show HN:"

**Title options (test 2-3):**
- "Show HN: I built a retro audio brand (5 products on Walmart)"
- "Show HN: Spherical UFO-style CD boombox — designed for 2026"
- "Show HN: Bringing back the spherical boombox form factor"

**First comment (critical — post this within 1 minute of the submission):**

```
Hey HN, I'm Brian, marketing lead at ByronStatics. We've spent the last
year building a small line of retro audio products — 5 SKUs total, all
on Walmart. I wanted to share a few of the design and product decisions
we made, and answer any questions.

The interesting one (to me) is the KBB-228 spherical CD boombox. There
aren't many spherical audio products in the world — the iconic reference
is the Panasonic RX-5050 from 1978. We tried to make a modern version
of that form factor, but with current CD + AM/FM + LCD tech, not a
single-function retro replica.

What we learned along the way:

1. The spherical form factor is harder to manufacture than rectangular.
   Tooling for the round shell is more expensive. We almost killed it
   twice for cost reasons. Glad we didn't.

2. The 70s/80s audio comeback is real and growing fast. We initially
   thought our target market was nostalgic 50-somethings. Our actual
   best customers are Gen Z digital-detoxers (25-35% of sales). They
   want a "no app, no notifications, no WiFi" experience.

3. We chose Walmart as the exclusive retailer intentionally. Amazon is
   the obvious choice, but Walmart's customer service reputation
   matched our brand positioning better. Curious what HN thinks about
   DTC-vs-marketplace for physical goods brands.

Tech stack note: the site is plain HTML + Tailwind + Cloudflare Pages.
No SPA, no React, no build step. Total page weight under 30KB. We
deliberately chose boring tech — the products are the brand, not
the website.

Happy to answer questions about the products, the design process,
or the retro audio market. Best, Brian.
```

### Common HN questions + how to answer

**"Why Walmart instead of building your own DTC site?"**
> Real answer: We're a small team (3 people). Building DTC means
> handling fulfillment, customer service, returns, fraud, chargebacks.
> Walmart absorbs all of that for a revenue share. The math is
> straightforward for our scale. When we hit $1M/year, we'll revisit.

**"Is this just a white-label Chinese product?"**
> The industrial design is ours. The manufacturing is in Asia (like
> basically every consumer audio brand). The product design, quality
> control, branding, and customer experience are ours. We visit the
> factory twice a year.

**"How do you compete with Victrola/Crosley?"**
> Different positioning. Victrola targets the turntable market.
> Crosley targets nostalgia kitsch. We're targeting the small but
> growing market of people who want real retro audio gear, not
> retro-LOOKING speakers with modern guts.

**"Spherical is gimmicky."**
> Maybe! It's not for everyone. But the people who love it really
> love it. We have customers who display the KBB-228 like a sculpture
> in their living room. That's a different relationship with audio
> gear than "another Bluetooth speaker."

**"Will you do a turntable?"**
> Eventually. We have 3 product concepts on the roadmap for 2027.
> Turntable is one of them, but the design has to be distinctive
> — not just another rectangular deck.

### How to handle a "buried" post

If after 2 hours your post is at page 3+:
- Reply to your own post with more context, technical detail
- Don't beg for upvotes (against HN rules)
- Don't resubmit (against HN rules)
- Try again in 2-3 weeks with a different angle

### The HN technical crowd loves:
- Stats (mention sales numbers, growth rates, customer segments)
- Honest admissions of failure / mistakes
- Specific technical details (build stack, page weight, time to first byte)
- Founders who are clearly building, not just pitching

### The HN crowd hates:
- Self-promotion
- Vague claims ("we're the #1 retro audio brand!")
- AI-generated content (be genuine)
- Astroturfing
- Posting in the wrong category

## Product Hunt launch

### When to launch
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best time:** 12:01 AM Pacific Time (PH runs on a 24-hour cycle starting at midnight PT)
- **Avoid:** Mondays, Fridays, weekends, holidays, big competitor launches
- **Launch calendar:** https://www.producthunt.com/launches

### Account prep
- Hunter account (post the product on behalf of the maker)
- Maker account (the brand account, comment + respond)
- Both need 30+ days of history

### Pre-launch checklist (1-2 weeks before)

- [ ] Create Maker profile with photo, bio, links
- [ ] Build a "coming soon" landing page for hunters to follow
- [ ] Get 5-10 friends/family to commit to upvote on launch day
- [ ] Reach out to power users in PH "Audio" / "Design" / "Tech" categories
- [ ] Build a list of 20+ PH followers who are active in the audio/design space
- [ ] Write the launch post in advance (see below)
- [ ] Prepare 5-7 product screenshots and 1 demo video

### The launch post

**Tagline (≤60 chars):**
> "Retro audio from the 70s & 80s, built for 2026"

**Description (~260 chars):**
> Five retro audio products — spherical CD boombox, classic 80s
> boombox, portable cassette player, portable CD player, and
> vintage AM/FM radio. All available on Walmart. Free shipping
> on orders over $35, 30-day returns, 1-year warranty.

**Topics/Categories:**
- Tech
- Audio
- Design
- Lifestyle
- Physical Product

**First comment (from the Maker account):**

```
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Brian, the marketing lead at ByronStatics. We've spent the last
year building a small line of retro audio products — 5 SKUs total,
all on Walmart. Today we're excited to share the whole collection
with the PH community.

Why retro audio? Two reasons:

1. **Privacy + simplicity.** No apps, no WiFi, no microphones, no
   always-on listening. Just a beautiful product that does one
   thing well.

2. **Design.** Modern audio gear all looks the same. Black
   rectangles, minimal buttons, generic plastic. We wanted to
   build products you'd display, not hide.

The flagship is the KBB-228 spherical CD boombox (pictured). It's
shaped like a UFO. The CD compartment flips up from the top. There
are 6 brass-feel control buttons. It's the only spherical CD
boombox on the market.

Curious to hear what you think. What retro audio product would YOU
want us to make next? Top ideas so far: turntable, 8-track player,
micro component system.

— Brian
```

### Hunter + Maker strategy

**Option A: Self-hunt (free, lower reach)**
- You launch the product from your own Maker account
- You hunt it yourself (allowed but signals less credibility)
- Expected: 100-300 upvotes, Top 10-20 in Audio category

**Option B: Find a Hunter (better reach)**
- Find a Product Hunt power user with 5K+ followers
- Ask them to hunt your product (they get credit)
- They're incentivized to make it succeed (their reputation)
- Expected: 300-1000 upvotes, Top 3-5 in Audio category

**How to find a Hunter:**
- Search PH for similar products (turntables, audio gear) — see who hunted them
- DM them: "Hey, we just launched ByronStatics retro audio gear — would you be interested in hunting it?"
- Offer: free product, shoutout, future collaboration

**Top PH Hunters to approach for audio/design products:**
- Chris Messina (@chrismessina) — design OG
- Ben Tossell (@bentossell) — maker community
- Kevin William David (@kevinwdavid) — PH hunter, 100+ products
- And 5-10 others via your own research

### The launch day (T+0 to T+24h)

**Hour 0 (12:01 AM PT):**
- Post the product
- Immediately comment as Maker with the launch story above
- Share the link in any relevant communities (Twitter, Discord, etc.)

**Hours 1-4:**
- Respond to every comment within 10 minutes
- Share in 3-5 relevant Twitter communities
- Ask power users to upvote (NOT friends/family spam)

**Hours 4-12:**
- Keep responding to comments
- Share in 2-3 more channels (LinkedIn, audio subreddits, etc.)
- Reply to any questions in comments with detail

**Hours 12-24:**
- Final engagement push
- Send "Last few hours" reminder to your engaged commenters
- Post a thank-you comment at Hour 23

**Day-after:**
- Thank everyone who upvoted
- Write a "Launch retrospective" blog post
- Save all comments for testimonial use

---

### PH day-of metrics to track

- **Upvotes** (target 200+ for Top 10 in category)
- **Comments** (target 30+ for organic boost)
- **Reviews/comments ratio** (high = good engagement signal)
- **Position in category** (target Top 5)
- **Daily rank** (target Top 20 overall)

---

### Worst case: bury in comments

If your post is underperforming by Hour 6:
- Engage MORE, not less
- Reply to every comment with substantive responses (5+ sentences)
- Ask open-ended questions to spark discussion
- Don't mention upvotes (against PH etiquette)

## Backup channels (in case HN/PH both fail)

### IndieHackers
- Better fit for early-stage brands than HN
- Audience: indie founders, bootstrappers
- Share revenue, growth, lessons
- More forgiving of promotional content

### Designer News
- Designer-focused Hacker News clone
- Smaller audience (50K) but highly engaged
- Good for design/visual content
- URL: news.designernews.co

### BetaList
- For upcoming launches
- Submit 2-4 weeks before launch
- Free submission, paid featured placement

### Launching Next
- Aggregator for product launches
- Free submission
- Smaller but targeted audience

## What to do if you go viral (good problem to have)

**If you hit 1K+ upvotes on HN or Top 5 on PH:**

1. **Pin the post** on your site (banner: "Featured on Product Hunt / Hacker News")
2. **Screenshot the metrics** (for case study, press, social)
3. **Follow up in 48 hours** with a "what we learned" post
4. **Capture every email** — set up a newsletter signup
5. **Engage every comment** for at least 1 week
6. **Pitch press** — "ByronStatics, the retro audio brand that hit Top 5 on Product Hunt, raises..."

---

## What to do if you don't go viral (most likely outcome)

**If your HN post buries and your PH launch is #15 in your category:**

1. **That's still 500-2000 visitors** — not bad for free
2. **You got 1-3 backlinks** from comments, press inquiries, etc.
3. **You learned what resonates** with the audience — apply to next launch
4. **Try again in 60-90 days** with a different angle (new product, milestone, etc.)

Most successful launches are the result of iteration. First launch is rarely the best.

## Realistic outcome expectations

### Hacker News
- **Likely outcome:** Buried at page 2-3, 200-500 visitors, 1-2 backlinks
- **Best case:** Front page for 2-4 hours, 5K-20K visitors, 5-10 backlinks
- **Worst case:** Removed by mods (against HN rules), 0 visitors

### Product Hunt
- **Likely outcome:** Top 5-10 in Audio category, 1K-3K visitors, 3-5 backlinks
- **Best case:** Top 5 overall, 10K-50K visitors, 20+ backlinks, podcast invites
- **Worst case:** Bottom of category, 200-500 visitors, 1-2 backlinks

Both outcomes are net positive. Just do it.
