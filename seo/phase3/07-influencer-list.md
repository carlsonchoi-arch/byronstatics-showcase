## What to offer influencers

### Standard offer (everyone gets)
- Free product (1 unit, $20-30 cost of goods)
- 1-year ByronStatics warranty
- Cross-promotion of their review on our channels

### Premium offer (top 3 creators only)
- Free product
- $200-500 cash payment (negotiable)
- Exclusive first-look at upcoming products
- Featured placement on our website

### What NOT to offer
- Affiliate revenue (illegal in some jurisdictions, complex)
- Equity (we're not raising)
- Future collaborations (vague, low value)

## Outreach timeline

### Week 1-2: Tier 2 outreach (5-10 creators)
- Lower competition for their attention
- Easier to get a response
- Smaller audience but more engaged

### Week 3-4: Tier 1 outreach (3-5 creators)
- Higher competition
- Need to be more compelling
- May take 1-2 weeks to get a response

### Week 5-8: Follow up + track results
- All packages should be in transit
- First reviews should start appearing
- Track sales from each creator (via UTMs)

### Week 9-12: Re-engage top performers
- The creators whose videos performed best
- Offer a second product
- Pitch ongoing collaboration
