## What is Buffer?

Buffer is a social media scheduling tool. Brian (Hermes) uses it to:
- Schedule posts in advance
- Manage 6+ platforms from one dashboard
- View analytics across all platforms
- Reply to comments from one inbox

**Free tier:** 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts at a time
**Pro tier ($6/mo):** Unlimited channels, unlimited posts, analytics, replies

## Phase B: Connect each platform (10 min)

### Step B1: Twitter
- In Buffer, click "Connect channel" → Twitter
- It'll redirect to Twitter OAuth
- Log in to @byronstatics
- Click "Authorize app"
- Buffer will pull your Twitter profile

### Step B2: Instagram
- Click "Connect channel" → Instagram
- Must use a **Business or Creator Instagram account** (not personal)
- If you set up a Business account (per the Instagram guide), this works
- Log in to Instagram
- Click "Authorize"
- Note: Instagram via Buffer only works for **single image posts and Reels** (no carousels as of writing)

### Step B3: Facebook Page
- Click "Connect channel" → Facebook
- Log in to your personal Facebook (Carlson's)
- Choose the **ByronStatics Page** (not your personal profile)
- Grant access to: publish posts, view insights, manage comments
- Buffer connects to the Page

### Step B4 (Pro only): LinkedIn
- Click "Connect channel" → LinkedIn
- Log in to your personal LinkedIn (Carlson's)
- Choose to connect as a **Company Page** (ByronStatics) or **Profile**
- Grant access

### Step B5 (Pro only): TikTok
- Click "Connect channel" → TikTok
- Log in to @byronstatics
- Grant access
- Note: TikTok via Buffer requires a Business account

### Step B6 (Pro only): YouTube
- Click "Connect channel" → YouTube
- Log in to your Google account (the one tied to the channel)
- Grant access
- YouTube Shorts via Buffer is supported

### Step B7 (Pro only): Pinterest
- Click "Connect channel" → Pinterest
- Log in to @byronstatics
- Grant access to: create pins, schedule pins, view analytics
- Buffer will pull your boards

## Phase C: Set posting schedule (2 min)

In Buffer, click "Settings" → "Posting schedule"

### Recommended posting times (US Eastern, since your audience is US)

| Platform | Best days | Best time |
|---|---|---|
| Twitter/X | Mon-Fri | 6-8 PM Eastern |
| Instagram | Mon-Sat | 6-9 PM Eastern |
| Facebook | Tue-Thu | 6-8 PM Eastern |
| LinkedIn | Tue-Thu | 8-10 AM Eastern |
| TikTok | Mon-Sat | 7-11 PM Eastern |
| YouTube Shorts | Mon, Wed, Fri | 5-9 PM Eastern |
| Pinterest | Any | Anytime (evergreen) |

⚠️ Carlson is in Asia/Shanghai TZ. The Buffer scheduler will store in Eastern or Pacific, but it'll publish at the right time for US audience. Brian will handle the conversion.

## Phase D: First scheduled post (bonus)

Once connected, create your first Buffer post:
1. Click "Compose"
2. Select channel (e.g., Twitter)
3. Write the post (use the first tweet from `01-reddit-posts.md` or `05-social-profile-bios.md`)
4. Upload image (KBB-228 hero)
5. Click "Schedule" → pick a date/time
6. Done
