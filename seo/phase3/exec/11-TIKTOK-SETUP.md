## Phase D: First 3 videos (7 min)

⚠️ TikTok rewards video from Day 1. You need at least 3 videos before the algorithm takes you seriously.

### How to film your first TikTok

**Option A: Use existing product photos + text overlay (fastest)**
1. Open Canva app
2. Use "TikTok Video" template
3. Add a product photo (KBB-228, AM66, etc.)
4. Add text overlay
5. Export as MP4
6. Upload to TikTok

**Option B: Film a real video (better for algorithm)**
1. Set up phone on tripod
2. Natural light (window or outdoor)
3. Film 60 sec of:
   - Product hero shot (3 sec)

## Phase E: Settings to tweak (1 min)

### Step E1: Privacy settings
- Settings → "Privacy"
- "Suggest your account to others" → ON
- "Allow others to duet" → ON (engagement)
- "Allow comments" → Everyone (filter later if needed)

### Step E2: Auto-caption
- Settings → "Accessibility" → "Always show captions" → ON

### Step E3: Watermark
- When you upload from phone, TikTok adds a watermark by default
- For better quality when re-uploading to other platforms: turn off "Save with watermark" in some versions
