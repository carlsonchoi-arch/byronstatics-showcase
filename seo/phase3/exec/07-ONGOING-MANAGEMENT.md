## 4. Content categories (the 5 types)

### Type 1: Product features (40% of content)
- 1 hero shot + 50-100 word caption
- Always link to product page or Walmart
- Example: "The KBB-228 is the only spherical CD boombox on the market. $24.99 on Walmart. #retroboombox"

### Type 2: Education / industry stats (20%)
- Share the +47% YoY CD growth stat
- Explain why retro audio is back
- 5 reasons to buy a CD player in 2026
- No direct product link (builds authority)

### Type 3: Social proof / reviews (20%)
- Screenshot 4-5 star reviews from Walmart
- Customer photos (with permission)
- "Best Seller" callouts
- 92 reviews at 4.1 stars = social proof

### Type 4: Lifestyle / aesthetic (15%)
- 70s living room setups
- Bedroom + AM66
- Beach + KBB-250
- No product link (engagement + brand building)

### Type 5: Community / engagement (5%)
- "Which is your favorite? 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5?"
- "Tag a friend who loves retro audio"
- "What was your first boombox?"
- Polls, questions, replies

**This 40/20/20/15/5 split keeps the feed interesting while still driving sales.**

## 5. Platform-specific guidelines

### Twitter/X
- **Posting frequency:** 1-2x/day
- **Best time:** 6-8 PM Eastern (6-8 AM China time)
- **Format:** 80% text, 20% images
- **Voice:** Casual, witty, retro-savvy
- **Hashtags:** 1-2 per tweet
- **Engagement:** Reply to every comment within 4 hours

### Instagram
- **Posting frequency:** 5-7x/week (1/day)
- **Best time:** 6-9 PM Eastern (6-9 AM China time)
- **Format:** 80% image, 20% carousel
- **Voice:** Visual-first, captions tell stories
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 per post
- **Stories:** Daily, 1-3 stories (behind-the-scenes, polls)
- **Reels:** 2-3x/week (re-use YouTube Shorts)

### Facebook
- **Posting frequency:** 1x/day
- **Best time:** 6-8 PM Eastern
- **Format:** 70% link, 30% image
- **Voice:** Slightly more formal than IG, brand-aware
- **Engagement:** Reply within 8 hours
- **Group sharing:** Share to relevant groups (Boomboxery, Retro Audio, etc.)

### LinkedIn
- **Posting frequency:** 3x/week (Tue/Wed/Thu)
- **Best time:** 8-10 AM Eastern (8-10 PM China time)
- **Format:** Text-first, professional
- **Voice:** Industry insights, brand-strategy
- **Engagement:** Reply to comments thoughtfully
- **Topics:** Retro audio market data, brand decisions, founder lessons

### TikTok
- **Posting frequency:** 3-5x/week
- **Best time:** 7-11 PM Eastern
- **Format:** 100% video, 60 sec max
- **Voice:** Casual, energetic, surprise-driven
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 per video
- **Sounds:** Use trending audio (helps algorithm)

### YouTube
- **Posting frequency:** 2-3 Shorts/week + 1 long-form/month (eventually)
- **Best time:** 5-9 PM Eastern
- **Format:** Vertical (9:16) Shorts, 1080x1920
- **Voice:** Slightly more polished than TikTok
- **SEO:** Title + description + tags with keywords

### Pinterest
- **Posting frequency:** 3-5 pins/day (first 4 weeks), then 1-3/day
- **Best time:** Any (Pinterest is evergreen search)
- **Format:** 2:3 vertical pins (1000x1500)
- **Voice:** SEO-optimized descriptions
- **Boards:** 12 boards (5 brand + 7 community)

## 9. Brand voice + style guide

### Voice attributes
- **Warm** — friendly, not corporate
- **Retro-savvy** — knows the difference between boombox eras
- **Design-conscious** — cares about how things look
- **Helpful** — answers questions thoroughly
- **Playful** — uses retro references, emojis when appropriate

### Words we use
- "far out" "groovy" "tubular" "stay golden" (occasional, on-brand)
- "spherical" "UFO-style" (KBB-228 only)
- "Walkman-style" (KCS-315 only)
- "Discman-style" (PCD-220 only)
- "rotary dial" "vintage" (AM66)

### Words we avoid
- "Cheap" "discount" (we say "affordable")
- "Just" (we don't use minimizing language)
- "Sorry" (replaced with "we'll make it right")
- "Guys" (use "everyone" or "folks" or "music lovers")

### Emojis we use
- 🎶 🎵 ☼ ✺ ★ (retro vibes)
- 👍 ✅ 🔗 (utility)
- 🛸 💿 📻 📟 (KBB-228 / CD / radio references)
- Avoid: 🙄 😂 💀 (too Gen Z slang for our brand)

### Tone examples

✅ **Good:** "The KBB-228 is the only spherical CD boombox on the market. UFO-inspired design meets modern CD + AM/FM sound. $24.99 on Walmart. ✺"

❌ **Bad:** "OMG guys look at this cool retro boombox!! It's only $25 😱 Link in bio 💕"

✅ **Good:** "We initially thought our target market was nostalgic 50-somethings. Our actual best customers are Gen Z digital-detoxers (25-35% of sales). The retro audio buyer has changed."

❌ **Bad:** "Did you know that Gen Z loves retro stuff too?? We were so surprised!"

## 10. Crisis management

### Red flags (escalate to Carlson + Kelly immediately)
- Defective product reports going viral
- Customer complaint with 100+ engagements
- Press inquiry (The Verge, Wired, NYT, etc.)
- Competitor attack / negative comparison post going viral
- Legal threat

### Yellow flags (handle with Brian, notify after)
- Single negative review (respond publicly, take offline)
- Customer service issue (resolve in DMs)
- Influencer controversy (unfollow + neutral statement)

### Green flags (handle with Brian only)
- Routine engagement
- Scheduled posts
- Analytics review
- Routine influencer outreach

### Crisis response template
```
"We hear you. [Acknowledge the issue]. Here's what we're doing: [action]. 
If you've been affected, please [specific step]. Thank you for the feedback."
```
