## Phase B: Page settings (10 min)

### Step B1: Settings → Page info
- Username: **byronstatics** (this gives you facebook.com/byronstatics)
- URL becomes: facebook.com/byronstatics

### Step B2: Settings → Templates and Tabs
- Choose "Shopping" or "Services" template (better for product pages)
- Add: "Shop" tab if you want to showcase products (optional)

### Step B3: Settings → Page Transparency
- Confirm Page is published
- Add admins: add your personal Facebook + 1 backup admin

### Step B4: Settings → Messaging
- Set "Messaging" to "On" (lets people DM you)
- Set up "Instant Replies" — auto-respond with: "Thanks for reaching out! We typically respond within 24 hours. For urgent inquiries, email info@thelivingenrichment.com"
- Add FAQ: "What is the warranty?" → "1 year. Contact us for any issues."

### Step B5: Set up action button
- Below cover photo, click "+ Add button"
- Choose "Shop Now" → Link to: https://www.walmart.com/seller/103033976/cp/shopall
- OR "Learn More" → Link to: https://byron-statics.com/

### Step B6: Instagram connection
- Settings → Accounts → Connect Instagram account
- Log in to your @byronstatics Instagram

### Step B7: Meta Business Suite
- Go to https://business.facebook.com/
- Add the Page to a Business Portfolio
- Name: "ByronStatics"
- This unlocks Meta Business Suite for analytics + later ad campaigns
