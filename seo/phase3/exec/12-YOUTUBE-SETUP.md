## Phase C: First Shorts upload (5 min)

### How to record your first YouTube Short

**Option A: Use existing product photos + text overlay (fastest)**
1. Open CapCut (free app)
2. New project → "9:16" (vertical)
3. Add product photos
4. Add text overlay + transitions
5. Add retro 70s background music (CapCut library, free)
6. Export as MP4 (1080 × 1920, 30fps)
7. Upload to YouTube as a Short

**Option B: Film a real video (better)**
1. Set up phone on tripod
2. Use natural light (window or outdoor)
3. Film 60 sec total
4. Edit in CapCut
5. Export + upload
