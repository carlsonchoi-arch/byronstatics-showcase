# Pinterest Strategy for ByronStatics

**Why Pinterest first:** It's the lowest-effort, highest-ROI channel for retro/vintage products. Pinterest users are actively searching for design inspiration, and the 70s aesthetic trends hard on the platform. 70s-themed Pins get 2-3x the impressions of generic product photos.

---

## Account setup (1-2 hours)

### Step 1: Create Pinterest Business account
- URL: business.pinterest.com
- Email: info@thelivingenrichment.com (one inbox for all brand comms)
- Display name: **ByronStatics**
- Username: @byronstatics
- Profile photo: ByronStatics logo (Pacifico wordmark, cream color on teal)
- Bio: "Far out audio from the 70s & 80s. Spherical boomboxes, portable CD players, cassette recorders, and vintage radios. A Vaughn Marketing Inc. brand."
- Website: https://byron-statics.com/
- Location: California, USA

### Step 2: Claim your website
- Settings → Claimed accounts → Enter byron-statics.com
- Add HTML meta tag (Pinterest provides it) OR upload HTML file
- Add to `<head>` of all pages: `<meta name="p:domain_verify" content="XXXXX"/>`

### Step 3: Enable Rich Pins
- Apply for Rich Pins (validates your site is the source)
- Approval: usually 1-2 days
- Once approved, product info from your site auto-populates pins

## Pin specs

### Image dimensions
- **Standard pin:** 1000 × 1500 px (2:3 ratio)
- **Square pin:** 1000 × 1000 px (for product shots)
- **Video pin:** 1000 × 1500 px, MP4, 6-15 seconds

### Text overlay (use Canva or Figma)
- Title: 5-8 words max, in retro font (Bagel Fat One or Bungee)
- Color: cream on teal OR cream on burnt orange
- 60% image, 40% text

### Pin descriptions (the SEO part!)
- 100-500 characters
- Include target keyword naturally: "spherical CD boombox", "retro boombox", etc.
- Include a clear call-to-action: "Shop the collection →", "Read the full guide →"
- 3-5 hashtags at the end: #retroboombox #70sdecor #vintagestyle #cdplayer #cassetteculture

## Targeting & SEO on Pinterest

Pinterest is essentially a visual search engine. Optimize for "Pinterest SEO":

### Board titles
Use the exact keyword phrases people search:
- "Retro Boombox" (not "Boombox Stuff")
- "Vintage AM FM Radio"
- "70s Home Decor"
- "Portable Cassette Player"

### Pin descriptions
- First sentence: target keyword
- Second sentence: benefit / what it is
- Third sentence: call-to-action with link

### Keywords to rank for (use 2-3 per pin)
- "spherical CD boombox"
- "retro boombox"
- "vintage boombox"
- "portable CD player"
- "AM FM radio vintage"
- "cassette player recorder"
- "70s decor"
- "retro home decor"
- "music lover gift"
- "vintage radio"

## Engagement strategy

### Group boards (join 5-10)
Pinterest group boards multiply your reach. Search for:
- "Retro Home Decor"
- "Vintage Audio"
- "70s Aesthetic"
- "Music Lovers"
- "Gift Ideas"

Join, follow board rules, contribute 1 pin per week.

### Pinterest Trends
Check Pinterest Trends weekly. Search "boombox", "cassette player", "70s decor" — note trending searches and create pins around them.

### Repin strategy
- Repin your own pins to other boards (allowed, encouraged)
- Save competitor pins (to monitor what works)
- Pin other people's content on your community boards (10:1 ratio of others:own)

## KPIs to track

### Pinterest Analytics
- Monthly views: target 5K by Day 30, 20K by Day 90
- Outbound clicks: target 100-500/month
- Saves per pin: 50+ = viral
- Followers: target 500 by Day 90

### UTM parameters (track in Plausible)
Use these for outbound links in pin descriptions:
```
?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=product
?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=blog
```

## Content batching workflow

### Monday morning (2 hours)
1. Take 5-10 new product photos (or use existing)
2. Open Canva, duplicate "Pin" template
3. Create 5 pins (1 per product)
4. Add descriptions with keywords
5. Schedule for the week via Pinterest native scheduler

### Monthly (1 hour)
- Refresh top-performing pins (update images, tweak titles)
- Add new seasonal content (Father's Day, Back to School, Holidays)
- Audit underperforming boards

## Common mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Pinning the same image to multiple boards (Pinterest de-dupes these)
- ❌ Generic stock photos (use real product photos)
- ❌ Skipping the description (the SEO is in the description)
- ❌ Pinning without a link (every pin should drive somewhere)
- ❌ Ignoring Rich Pins (apply for them — free SEO boost)
