# YouTube Shorts Scripts for ByronStatics

**Why YouTube Shorts:** They have viral potential like TikTok but better long-term SEO value (YouTube videos rank in Google search). One Short with 100K+ views = real backlinks, real brand awareness, real Walmart sales.

**Format:** 60 seconds max. Vertical (9:16). Filmed on iPhone is fine. No editing required — just good content.

**Critical:** The first 3 seconds MUST hook the viewer. If you don't grab them, they swipe.

---

## Filming setup

### Equipment (all you need)
- iPhone (any model from 12+ works)
- Tripod or phone stand (~$15)
- Natural light (window or outdoor)
- White/cream background for product shots
- Optional: ring light (~$20)
- Optional: lavalier mic (~$15)

### Filming locations (5 to rotate)
1. **Hero setup** — white background, well-lit, product centered
2. **Lifestyle** — living room or bedroom, product in context
3. **Hand-held demo** — walk-and-talk with product
4. **Outdoor** — patio, beach, park (great for KBB-250)
5. **Workshop/desk** — tech setup, multiple products visible

### Editing tools
- **CapCut** (free, mobile + desktop) — best for Shorts
- **iMovie** (free, Mac/iOS) — basic but works
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free, desktop) — pro level

### Music
- Use trending audio from YouTube Shorts (helps algorithm)
- Or royalty-free retro 70s music (Epidemic Sound, Artlist)
- NEVER use copyrighted music without license

## Filming workflow

### Pre-production (30 min per video)
1. Write script (use templates above)
2. Charge phone
3. Set up backdrop
4. Test audio levels
5. Frame each shot

### Production (15-30 min per video)
1. Film 3 takes of each scene
2. Film B-roll (close-ups, lifestyle shots)
3. Record voiceover separately (clearer audio)
4. Total runtime: 15-30 minutes

### Post-production (30-45 min per video)
1. Edit in CapCut
2. Add text overlays
3. Add background music
4. Add intro/outro (optional — Shorts work better without)
5. Export 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps

### Publishing (10 min per video)
1. Upload to YouTube
2. Add title, description, tags
3. Add thumbnail (auto-generated by YouTube)
4. Schedule for peak time
5. Cross-post to TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest

### Total per video: ~1.5-2 hours
For 2 videos/week: 3-4 hours/week
For 3 videos/week: 5-6 hours/week

## Batch filming strategy

**Film 5 Shorts in one session (3-4 hours)**
- Set up all 5 products
- Film all 5 hero shots
- Film all 5 lifestyle shots
- Film all 5 voiceovers
- Edit later in the week (1-2 hours per video)

This is way more efficient than filming one Short per day.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- ❌ Filming vertically in low light (grainy)
- ❌ Talking for 5+ seconds without a cut (people swipe)
- ❌ Using copyrighted music
- ❌ No text overlays (most viewers watch muted)
- ❌ Slow pacing — Shorts need a new visual every 2-3 seconds
- ❌ Forgetting the CTA — always include "Link in bio" or "Walmart"
