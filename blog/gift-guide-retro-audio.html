Gift Guide: Retro Audio for the Music Lover Who Has Everything

They've got AirPods Max. They have Sonos in every room. What do you get the person who seems to have it all? A retro audio gift. Here's our 2026 guide.

Why retro audio makes the perfect gift

Most audio gifts fall flat. Headphones? They already have their favorite pair. A speaker? It's just another gadget. Subscriptions? They probably have those too.

Retro audio is different. It triggers something deeper — nostalgia, personality, and a sense of physical ownership. A retro CD boombox or vintage radio is the kind of gift that ends up on a shelf, talked about, and kept for years. It becomes part of the home.

Plus, retro audio gear is genuinely affordable. All of our picks are under $50.
